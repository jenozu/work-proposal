# Commercialization Strategy

**Internal problem → working internal tool → measured results → reusable product → external pilot → commercial offer.**

Possible offers: one-time custom implementation; recurring licence; or implementation plus recurring hosting/support. Not every internal tool should become SaaS. Smaller, specialized workflows may be better as project-based services.

Before external commitments, validate: employer/IP ownership; market demand; willingness to pay; portability beyond MARINDUSTRIAL; contractual access to ERP/carrier systems; security; tenancy; onboarding; documentation; maintenance; support coverage; and unit economics.

Keep three figures separate: internal operating benefit, potential external **gross revenue**, and potential external **contribution after commercial costs**. A revenue scenario is not a sales forecast.
