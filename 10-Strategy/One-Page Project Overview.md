# Proposed Projects — One-Page Overview
**MARINDUSTRIAL | Operations Improvement & Automation Initiative**

## Purpose
Identify repetitive operational work, improve existing processes and measure the results. Start by evaluating projects with work already underway or a clearly defined need; expand only when their costs and benefits justify further investment.

## A. Initial Projects
*Six opportunities for individual evaluation—not six simultaneous implementation commitments.*

| Project | Proposed improvement | Current position / next step |
|---|---|---|
| **RF Scanner / Warehouse Management** | Streamline inventory lookup, receiving, putaway, picking and inventory counting. | Existing development project; **first candidate for a 30-day pilot**, subject to approval and SAP test-server access. |
| **Automated Shipping Notifications** | Send shipment confirmations and tracking information automatically, reducing manual emails and potentially reducing status inquiries. | Proposed concept; confirm workflow, data availability and potential time savings. |
| **Invoice Retrieval** | Search approved Outlook/document sources for supplier invoices by invoice number, vendor or purchase order. | Previous prototype; verify current functionality, access permissions and search accuracy. |
| **Shipping Automation** | Simplify shipment preparation and reduce repeated carrier data entry, with future multi-carrier capabilities where feasible. | Purolator batch-shipping prototype exists; verify its current capabilities and integration needs. |
| **Commercial Invoice Generator** | Prepare international shipping documents from a few inputs and reusable product information, with review of customs details. | Defined concept; validate data sources and document-approval controls. |
| **Landed Cost Automation** | Match freight and brokerage charges with purchase orders and goods receipts to simplify reconciliation. | Existing tool; adapt its data workflow to current SAP access and verify matching accuracy. |

## B. Future Operational Improvements
Additional opportunities include purchasing and inventory analytics, management dashboards, SAP workflow improvements, a customer order-status portal, internal task management and document automation. These form a **future backlog**, to be evaluated using the same cost-benefit approach rather than developed all at once.

## C. Potential New Revenue
If an internal tool demonstrates measurable value, MARINDUSTRIAL could assess whether it can be adapted for other distributors or warehouses through **software licensing, implementation or ongoing support**. Customer demand, ownership, productization costs and support requirements would need separate validation. Hypothetical external revenue is **not included** in the internal financial justification.

## Immediate Request
Approve a **30-day RF Scanner pilot** once SAP test-server access and the appropriate integration permissions are available. Confirm the integration licence and its terms (currently a **provisional estimate of approximately CAD $100/month, billed annually**) and account for staff testing/development time. Measure before-and-after warehouse workflows, then present actual results and an updated cost-benefit analysis **before** making broader investment decisions.

*Project descriptions reflect the proposal's working documentation. Prototype stages and expected benefits require verification; none of the listed benefits is presented as a measured result.*
