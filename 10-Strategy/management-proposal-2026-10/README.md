# Management proposal — internal development capability (October 2026)

**Status:** revision for management consideration; not approved for rollout.  
**Purpose:** obtain authorization for a controlled 60-day evaluation of internal software development and automation as an operational improvement capability.

## Executive recommendation

Marindustrial can test whether targeted internal software development improves the consistency, efficiency and accuracy of existing operations. The long-term decision is **not** whether five applications can be built in isolation. It is whether an accountable internal capability could continuously identify inefficiencies, prioritize work, safely deliver appropriate solutions, support users, maintain deployed software and demonstrate recurring business value.

Management is asked to approve a **bounded 60-day evaluation involving one or two selected pilots**, with a documented time allocation, named operational sponsor, access subject to IT/SAP permissions and a formal day-60 findings review. This does **not** request an immediate permanent role, broad SAP write access or simultaneous deployment of five projects.

## Five priority projects — current scope

1. **Automated Inventory Cycle Counting.** Evaluate recurring ABC counting and variance follow-up without assuming that annual physical counts can be eliminated. Provisional intervals A/B/C = 30/90/180 calendar days; policy remains subject to testing and Finance/management approval.
2. **Centralized Reporting & Automated BI.** Define trustworthy, actionable KPIs and reduce repeated manual report preparation through controlled SAP-compatible data access.
3. **Automated Batch Shipping & SAP Integration.** Pilot repeatable label and delivery workflows, subject to carrier/SAP access, duplicate prevention, exception handling and manual fallback. Processing-time targets are hypotheses, not delivered savings.
4. **Warehouse Slotting & Picking Optimization.** Rank item placement by actual physical picking frequency—not sales revenue—while respecting item size, safety and handling constraints. Compare simulated and measured travel before limited relocation.
5. **RF Scanner & Warehouse Management System.** Validate existing code-supported warehouse modules separately from proposed functionality and production-approved SAP transactions.

The former priority project, **Automated Direct-Ship Processing & SAP Invoice Management**, moves to the beginning of the near-term future-opportunities tier. It is not a sixth priority pilot.

## Decision model

Compare three options fairly: continue manual workflows; purchase/outsource available solutions; or build an internal development and maintenance capability. The correct answer depends on the specific workflow, fully loaded costs, support needs, delivery risk, product fit and measured recurring demand.

A future developer role should be based on a **managed portfolio** rather than a single tool's savings: requirements, version control, security, testing, training, user support, maintenance, lifecycle documentation, adoption and an approved backlog are ongoing work.

## Evidence controls

Separate historical records, reported operational baselines, observed timed tests, code/prototype status, calculations, projections and unverified assumptions. Released employee hours are operational capacity unless a documented expense is avoided. Deduplicate benefits shared by cycle counting, RF workflows, warehouse slotting and shipping.

Do not treat working repo documents as proof of production deployment; linked implementation repositories, tests and operations owners must confirm live status. Do not publish company/customer/vendor identifiers, raw SAP exports, prices or other confidential management evidence in this public repository without employer authorization.

**Companion management deliverables** (Word/PDF, full evidence register, management Q&A and calculations) are retained outside this public repository pending approved distribution.
