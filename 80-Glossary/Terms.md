# Plain-Language Terms

- **Automation:** software performs a repetitive step that an employee would otherwise do manually.
- **CBA / cost-benefit analysis:** structured comparison of a project's incremental cost and measurable benefit.
- **Usable capacity:** time released by a tool that staff can productively redirect; not automatically a cash saving.
- **Cash saving:** a verified reduction in actual expenditure or avoided incremental expenditure.
- **Payback:** time required for net benefit to recover the initial incremental investment.
- **WMS:** warehouse management system supporting receiving, putaway, stock visibility, picking and counting.
- **RF Scanner:** the existing mobile warehouse application being evaluated in this proposal.
- **GRPO:** goods receipt purchase order, the SAP Business One document representing goods received against purchasing.
- **Recurring revenue / ARR:** gross subscription revenue in a twelve-month period at an assumed active customer count; not profit.
- **Scenario:** an intentionally hypothetical example; not a forecast or actual result.
- **Loaded labour rate:** hourly labour cost including assumed employment burden; verify with Finance before using in approved CBAs.
