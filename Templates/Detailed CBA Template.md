# [Tool] — Detailed CBA

1. Scope and current workflow, including owner and baseline frequency.
2. Raw timing protocol and representative sample characteristics.
3. Before/after comparison and error/correction rates.
4. Transparent annual hours calculation with assumptions.
5. Usable capacity vs actual cash savings, separated and non-overlapping.
6. Remaining build, training, integration, equipment and annual running costs.
7. Net internal operational value, sensitivity range and payback (if positive).
8. Vendor alternative **only when comparable scoped quotations exist**.
9. External customer/price scenario **separately** with gross revenue and cost of service.
10. Dependencies, risks, contradictory results, confidence labels and evidence.
11. Pilot conclusion and recommended decision questions.

Method: [[Cost-Benefit Methodology]]. Do not claim accuracy or ROI for inputs not yet measured.
