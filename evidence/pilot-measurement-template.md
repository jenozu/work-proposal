| Decision | Continue, revise, pause or stop |

## Computation when measured

**Gross annual handling time released** = (mean baseline seconds − mean assisted seconds) × verified annual volume ÷ 3,600.

**Net labour capacity** further considers review, exceptions, adoption and any displaced tasks; do not assume 100% recoverability. Cash savings or expense avoidance require a demonstrated spend change, not merely equivalent hourly wage.

## Pilot approval checkpoint

- Scope and success criteria agreed.
- Test data and system access approved.
- Correctness and reconciliation verified by process owner.
- Exception handling and rollback tested.
- Ongoing support owner identified.
- Results independently reviewed before branch-wide rollout.
