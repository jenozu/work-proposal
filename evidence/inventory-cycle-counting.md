# Automated Inventory Cycle Counting — Evidence and Open Questions

## Historical and preliminary observations

- Annual count: approximately **4 people × 2–3 eight-hour days = 64–96 direct employee-hours**, excluding preparation, post-count investigation and operational disruption.
- Reported count-line accuracy: **2023: 97.36%**, **2024: 94.72%**, **2025: 91.41%**. Confirm comparable SAP definitions, dates and eligibility.
- Initial analysis identified **3,833 stocked item records**. An older partial grouping of A: 87, B: 304, C: 2,358 totals **2,749**, leaving **1,084** outside that grouping. Do not extrapolate the categories as full item coverage.
- Preliminary **15–20 minutes per working day** is an unvalidated physical-count concept, not a complete recurring-labour estimate.

## Current provisional cadence to test

| Category | Working interval | Important caveat |
|---|---|---|
| A | **30 calendar days** | Engines/PTOs may be grouped and quick to verify but require adequate controls |
| B | **90 calendar days** | Large or boxed parts may count faster than simple class assumptions suggest |
| C | **365 calendar days** | Small fasteners and loose items can take longer than their value suggests |

These are **proposed parameters**, not final company policy. Eligibility and category priority may reflect stock exposure, movement, criticality, lead times and discrepancy history; physical count effort also matters. Calendar intervals must translate to real workdays.

## What to validate

Full count-item universe and bin-location coverage; physical time by product and packaging type; localized count-area controls; travel; interruptions; variance investigations, recounts and adjustments; SAP permissions; Finance/auditor year-end counting requirements.

**Caution:** Counting throughout the year may take equal or greater labour than an annual shutdown. Stronger preliminary objectives are inventory accuracy, earlier discrepancy detection and operational continuity. These numbers are working historical summaries, not signed-off operational results.
