# Direct shipping and SAP invoice attachment — supporting research

## Two distinct activities

1. **Interbranch direct shipping:** Another branch fulfils a Mississauga customer's sales order and invoices Mississauga. The local process must match documents and complete the appropriate purchasing/receiving and customer invoicing workflow. Confirm serial numbers, quantity, product identity, branch charges and transaction rules.
2. **Invoice PDF management:** Locate supplier invoice documents, validate identifiers, attach the correct PDF to the correct SAP transaction or record, and deal with duplicates and exceptions.

They share document-matching and SAP navigation requirements but should be **timed separately**. No measured per-case baseline or annual volume for direct shipping or SAP document attachment has been established in the management analysis.

## Related historical document-retrieval study (not SAP attachment savings)

A separate 12-month Outlook/vendor document analysis reported:
- **4,516** Outlook messages scanned.
- **828** messages matching selected senders.
- **1,160** matching PDFs.
- **1,074** unique document candidates after **86** duplicates.
- Approximately **30 seconds** previously spent saving one invoice manually.

These figures concern **document retrieval and filing**, not necessarily the number of SAP invoice attachments or interbranch direct-ship transactions. They must **not** be combined with or mislabelled as proven Project 4 processing savings. They are included only as a feasibility and adjacent-workflow reference; source data remain private.
