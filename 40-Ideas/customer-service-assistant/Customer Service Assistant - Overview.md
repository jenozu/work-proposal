# Customer Service Assistant

**Group:** Customer  
**Working stage:** concept. This is a proposal status, not a verification of the current code/deployment.

## Current inefficiency
Employees answer the same non-sensitive customer questions repeatedly.

## Proposed improvement
Provide source-backed responses to approved FAQs and route complex inquiries to employees.

## Testable measures
Eligible inquiries; handoff quality; response time; corrections; satisfaction.

## External possibility
Configured service assistant and maintenance.

## Boundaries and open questions
No unsupported order-specific answers or disclosure without identity verification.

See [[Customer Service Assistant - Working CBA]] for the project-specific financial evidence checklist. Before proceeding, identify workflow owner, frequency, existing cost, initial investment and operational dependencies.
