# Shipment Notification Automation

**Group:** Customer  
**Working stage:** previously discussed concept. This is a proposal status, not a verification of the current code/deployment.

## Current inefficiency
Manual tracking emails and repetitive status inquiries consume employee time.

## Proposed improvement
Send approved customer shipment notifications with order and tracking details after shipment.

## Testable measures
Minutes per email; eligible shipment volume; delivery accuracy; changes in status calls.

## External possibility
One-time integration plus maintenance or hosted subscription.

## Boundaries and open questions
Do not count fewer customer calls without measuring actual reduction and avoiding overlap with the portal.

## Financial note
See [[Shipment Notification Automation - Working CBA]] for the project-specific financial evidence checklist. Before proceeding, identify workflow owner, frequency, existing cost, initial investment and operational dependencies.
