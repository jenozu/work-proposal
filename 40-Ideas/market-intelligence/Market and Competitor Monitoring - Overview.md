# Market and Competitor Monitoring

**Group:** Growth  
**Working stage:** concept. This is a proposal status, not a verification of the current code/deployment.

## Current inefficiency
Relevant competitor and market changes are time-consuming to track manually.

## Proposed improvement
Summarize approved public market sources and notify staff of relevant developments.

## Testable measures
Research hours; verified findings; documented decisions supported.

## External possibility
Custom research reporting service subject to source rights.

## Boundaries and open questions
Respect source terms and review uncertain AI summaries before distribution.

## Financial note
See [[Market and Competitor Monitoring - Working CBA]] for the project-specific financial evidence checklist. Before proceeding, identify workflow owner, frequency, existing cost, initial investment and operational dependencies.
