# Product Affinity and Cross-Selling

**Group:** Sales  
**Working stage:** concept. This is a proposal status, not a verification of the current code/deployment.

## Current inefficiency
Related purchase patterns can be hard to identify manually across many products.

## Proposed improvement
Identify frequently co-purchased items and show sales staff potential follow-up products.

## Testable measures
Suggestions reviewed; accepted cross-sells; attributable incremental gross margin.

## External possibility
Analytics add-on for industrial sellers and wholesalers.

## Boundaries and open questions
Avoid treating correlations as guaranteed sales opportunities.

## Financial note
See [[Product Affinity and Cross-Selling - Working CBA]] for the project-specific financial evidence checklist. Before proceeding, identify workflow owner, frequency, existing cost, initial investment and operational dependencies.
