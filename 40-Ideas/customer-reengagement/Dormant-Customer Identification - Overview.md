# Dormant-Customer Identification

**Group:** Sales  
**Working stage:** concept. This is a proposal status, not a verification of the current code/deployment.

## Current inefficiency
Previously active customers may reduce or stop purchasing without systematic follow-up.

## Proposed improvement
Flag meaningful purchasing declines and prepare salesperson follow-up queues.

## Testable measures
Accounts flagged and reviewed; responses; incremental gross margin from attributable wins.

## External possibility
Sales analytics module or project-based data service.

## Boundaries and open questions
Incremental revenue must use gross margin, not gross sales, in net financial benefit.

## Financial note
See [[Dormant-Customer Identification - Working CBA]] for the project-specific financial evidence checklist. Before proceeding, identify workflow owner, frequency, existing cost, initial investment and operational dependencies.
