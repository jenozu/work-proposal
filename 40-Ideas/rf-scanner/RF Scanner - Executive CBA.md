# RF Scanner — Executive Cost-Benefit Summary

**Evaluation:** proposed 60-day internal pilot. **Currency:** CAD. **All figures below are illustrative planning assumptions, not measured MARINDUSTRIAL results.**

## Problem and solution
Warehouse employees spend time finding stock, receiving and putting products away, picking separate orders, and completing inventory counts. The RF Scanner aims to connect these activities through mobile inquiry, receiving/staging, guided putaway, wave picking, inventory counting and movement history.

## Internal value (illustrative)
| Metric | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Annual employee hours released | 216 hours |
| Equivalent labour capacity at $30/hour | $6,480 |
| Usable capacity assumption | 75% |
| **Annual usable capacity value** | **$4,860** |
| Annual operating and maintenance | ($1,860) |
| **Annual net operational value** | **$3,000** |

Released capacity is **not** a reduction in payroll unless it leads to verified avoided expenditure.

## Required investment
| Metric | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Additional development (80h at $40) | $3,200 |
| Training / testing (12h at $30) | $360 |
| **Initial remaining investment** | **$3,560** |
| Illustrative payback based on operational value | **14.2 months** |

## Potential external value (separate scenario)
Five hypothetical customers paying $250/month could generate **$15,000 annual recurring gross revenue**; five hypothetical $1,500 setups could add **$7,500 one-time gross revenue**. The **$22,500 gross first-year** scenario assumes all five pay for twelve months and excludes commercial development, support, acquisition and other costs. It is **not a forecast**.

## Pilot
Measure real lookup, picking **including sorting**, receiving/putaway and count times; validate costs and quantify corrections. Update this summary from [[RF Scanner - Detailed CBA]] at the end of [[60-Day Pilot Plan]].
