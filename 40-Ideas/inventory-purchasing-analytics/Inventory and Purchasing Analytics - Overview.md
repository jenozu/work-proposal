# Inventory and Purchasing Analytics

**Group:** Analytics  
**Working stage:** concept. This is a proposal status, not a verification of the current code/deployment.

## Current inefficiency
Sales and inventory history is difficult to translate into timely purchasing decisions.

## Proposed improvement
Surface stockouts, slow movers, excess stock, usage trends and possible reorder exceptions.

## Testable measures
Analyst time; stockout rate; excess stock; avoided urgent purchases where attributable.

## External possibility
Recurring reporting subscription or implementation service.

## Boundaries and open questions
Present recommendations for purchasing review rather than automatically placing POs.

## Financial note
See [[Inventory and Purchasing Analytics - Working CBA]] for the project-specific financial evidence checklist. Before proceeding, identify workflow owner, frequency, existing cost, initial investment and operational dependencies.
