# Receiving and Putaway

**Group:** Warehouse  
**Working stage:** planned RF module or possible standalone offering. This is a proposal status, not a verification of the current code/deployment.

## Current inefficiency
Manual receipt verification and unclear tracking between arrival and storage.

## Proposed improvement
Import open purchase orders, verify deliveries, record exceptions, stage received stock and guide putaway by bin route.

## Testable measures
Receipt completion time; putaway time; unlocated stock; overages/shortages; correction effort.

## External possibility
Focused receiving module for warehouses that do not require a full WMS.

## Boundaries and open questions
Module of the RF Scanner in the internal business case. Do not count the same time savings twice.

## Financial note
See [[Receiving and Putaway - Working CBA]] for the project-specific financial evidence checklist. Before proceeding, identify workflow owner, frequency, existing cost, initial investment and operational dependencies.
