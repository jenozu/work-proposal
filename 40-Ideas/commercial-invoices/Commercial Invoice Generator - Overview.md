# Commercial Invoice Generator

**Group:** Shipping  
**Working stage:** previously specified concept. This is a proposal status, not a verification of the current code/deployment.

## Current inefficiency
Repetitive paperwork for warranty returns and international shipments.

## Proposed improvement
Enter claim number, part and weight; reuse approved product descriptions and reviewed tariff/origin information; generate export documents.

## Testable measures
Minutes per invoice; missing-field corrections; classification review effort.

## External possibility
Export paperwork tool with configuration and update support.

## Boundaries and open questions
HS classification, origin and customs statements require verification; automated suggestions are not definitive.

## Financial note
See [[Commercial Invoice Generator - Working CBA]] for the project-specific financial evidence checklist. Before proceeding, identify workflow owner, frequency, existing cost, initial investment and operational dependencies.
