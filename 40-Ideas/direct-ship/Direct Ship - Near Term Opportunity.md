# Automated Direct-Ship Processing & SAP Invoice Management

**Tier:** near-term future opportunity; not a current priority pilot.

The concept is to reduce repeat handling across direct-ship purchase/sales transaction preparation, approved invoice searches, consistent document filing and reconciliation to relevant SAP Business One records.

Earlier planning notes report invoice retrieval/file-handling prototype work, but the scope, production deployment, transaction-posting capability and authorizations are **not independently verified**. A future discovery phase should inspect available code and workflows and establish an approved baseline.

**Potential benefit:** more consistent document associations, reduced duplicate entry and fewer missing-document follow-ups, provided the system operates within mailbox and ERP permissions.

**Gate:** assign an operations and Finance owner, confirm transaction scope and document-access rules, measure current error/rework and handling time, test exception handling with synthetic or approved samples, and avoid automated SAP posting before IT/SAP acceptance.

Prioritization should be reviewed after the first 60-day pilot results; this appendix entry does not request approval to deploy direct-ship automation.
