# Customer Order-Status Portal

**Group:** Customer  
**Working stage:** concept. This is a proposal status, not a verification of the current code/deployment.

## Current inefficiency
Customers need employees to answer repetitive questions about orders, tracking and invoices.

## Proposed improvement
Secure self-service portal with approved status, tracking and document access.

## Testable measures
Status requests per month; employee minutes per request; self-service use; customer satisfaction.

## External possibility
Configured customer portal service for distributors.

## Boundaries and open questions
Customer authorization, current data and secure access are prerequisites.

## Financial note
See [[Customer Order-Status Portal - Working CBA]] for the project-specific financial evidence checklist. Before proceeding, identify workflow owner, frequency, existing cost, initial investment and operational dependencies.
