# Website and Lead-Capture Improvements

**Group:** Growth  
**Working stage:** concept. This is a proposal status, not a verification of the current code/deployment.

## Current inefficiency
Digital visitors may not find product information or a simple way to request a quote.

## Proposed improvement
Improve product-facing pages, request flows and measurable lead tracking.

## Testable measures
Qualified leads; quote conversion; incremental attributable gross margin; implementation cost.

## External possibility
Potential custom digital workflow service rather than necessarily reusable software.

## Boundaries and open questions
Separate general website work from the internal automation pilot if scope becomes too broad.

See [[Website and Lead-Capture Improvements - Working CBA]] for the project-specific financial evidence checklist. Before proceeding, identify workflow owner, frequency, existing cost, initial investment and operational dependencies.
