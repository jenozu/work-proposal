NotebookLM will not automatically ingest every file in a GitHub folder or stay synced solely because a repo link is given. Import supported individual files or compatible documents, refresh imported content when updated, and use source links and citations to check its conclusions.

## Useful questions for NotebookLM

- Compare traditional deterministic automation with AI-agent workflows across the five pilots. Which tasks genuinely benefit from language models, and which should stay conventional Python/SAP integration or optimization code?
- Design an orchestrated read-only reporting exception assistant: inputs, isolated tools, citations, structured outputs, human review, retries, audit trail, cost and success metrics.
- Propose bounded agent roles for approved document matching and knowledge retrieval. How should uncertain output be escalated rather than posted to SAP?
- Identify reusable foundations shared by the RF Scanner, warehouse slotting simulator and reporting pipeline, without double counting projected operational benefits.
- Rank small, synthetic-data proof-of-concept experiments that could be built with Codex and tested locally in PowerShell.

## Data and privacy

This repo is **public**. Do not upload raw Marindustrial SAP workbooks, inventory item/value exports, invoices, customer information, credentials or proprietary operational details to public research sources or third-party AI products unless explicit company authorization and acceptable service settings are confirmed. Begin with synthetic or approved non-sensitive inputs.

AI-generated reports, NotebookLM answers and demonstrations **are not evidence** that Marindustrial approved the project or that an SAP integration works. Preserve an independent evidence trail and require human review for operational changes.
