# 1. Executive Summary

Marindustrial Inc. has an opportunity to improve operational efficiency through internally developed software tailored to existing business processes. Warehouse, inventory, reporting, shipping and administrative work can involve repeated entry, document handling and switching between applications. A dedicated internal software-development capability could complement SAP Business One 10.0, reduce unnecessary work and support improvements across Mississauga, Montreal, Dartmouth and Moncton.

Five initial pilots illustrate this potential: **Automated Inventory Cycle Counting; Centralized Reporting & Automated Business Intelligence; Automated Batch Shipping & SAP Integration; Warehouse Slotting & Picking Optimization; and RF Scanner & Warehouse Management System.** These projects are examples of what an ongoing Software Developer could identify, build, maintain and improve—not a one-time list of five applications. Direct-Ship Processing & SAP Invoice Management has moved to the near-term future backlog.

Preliminary research suggests that several processes warrant measured testing. Annual physical inventory counts have involved about **four employees for two to three working days** and can interrupt normal operations. Shipping administration currently takes approximately **75 seconds per shipment** in the observed workflow versus a **proposed**, unvalidated 10–15-second assisted target. A reported 12-month shipment total of **2,020 completed shipments** implies a scenario of **33.7–36.5 hours of handling time released per year if every shipment were eligible and the target achieved**—not a confirmed saving. A weekly service-report process is preliminarily estimated at 1–2 hours of manual work. Actual pick frequency, travel savings, RF productivity and recurring maintenance costs are not yet established.

The immediate recommendation is controlled pilot testing: document baseline work, develop limited solutions, verify correctness and security, and compare end-to-end processes—including exceptions, employee review and operating cost. Recovered time is employee capacity, not necessarily a cash or payroll reduction. Larger investments should depend on evidence rather than hypothetical ROI.

**Management approval requested:** authorize a supervised, limited-scope evaluation with defined process owners, reasonable test access, measurable success criteria and separate approval for spending. If trials demonstrate repeatable, sustainable value, Marindustrial could consider a dedicated internal **Software Developer** role to integrate and maintain successful applications and pursue further opportunities across departments.
