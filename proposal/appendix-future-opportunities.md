# Appendix — Future Opportunities (Optional)

This is an **illustrative, prioritized opportunity backlog**, not an approved implementation schedule. The five management pilots remain the current priority. Tiers indicate likely sequencing and readiness for *evaluation*, not proven ROI or promised delivery dates.

## Tier 1 — Near-term: existing foundations and adjacent workflows

| Opportunity | Intended operational value |
|---|---|
| **1. Automated Direct-Ship Processing & SAP Invoice Management** ([details](backlog/01-direct-ship-invoice-management.md)) | Guide interbranch SAP processing and invoice matching/attachment; reportedly partially built, must be verified |
| Supplier Invoice Retrieval & Filing | Identify approved invoice emails and PDFs, name and file consistently, manage duplicates |
| GRPO / Supplier Invoice Comparator | Flag quantity, price, PO and invoice mismatches for purchasing review |
| Weekly Service & Departmental Report Automation | Expand proven scheduled reporting after validating the first service-report pilot |
| Shipment Notifications & Tracking | Prepare controlled shipping alerts and delivery information |
| Commercial Invoices & Customs Documents | Reuse validated shipment inputs in export paperwork with human checks |
| Receiving, Putaway & Wave Picking Extensions | Expand RF processes and measured route assistance only after corresponding pilots |
| SAP Document Attachment & Lookup | Reduce repeated SAP navigation and authorized file matching |

## Prioritization and safeguards

Start with measurable repeated effort, existing code, low-risk access, user demand and maintenance feasibility. Reassess each tier based on evidence rather than technology novelty. Potential time released is not automatic cash savings. AI agents/orchestration are **exploratory implementation methods** and must not become a sixth management pilot without a new decision.
