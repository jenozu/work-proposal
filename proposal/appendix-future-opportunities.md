# Appendix — Future Opportunities (Optional)

**Backlog only.** The five proposed pilots remain the primary scope. This list illustrates a potential longer-term internal development pipeline, **not** approved work or forecast savings.

| Opportunity | Example workflow |
|---|---|
| Supplier invoice retrieval and filing | Retrieve authorized invoice attachments, identify matching purchase orders, file documents consistently |
| GRPO / supplier invoice comparison | Compare item quantities, prices and vendor references for review before SAP posting |
| Receiving, putaway and wave picking | Extend barcode and route-assisted warehouse workflows after RF pilot validation |
| Shipment notifications | Generate consistent outbound notifications and tracking details |
| Commercial invoice / shipping documents | Reduce repeated entry for international shipment paperwork with human verification |
| Purchasing and inventory analytics | Review reorder needs, slow movers, shortages and turnover |
| Service and operations reporting | Automate scheduled departmental metrics and exception reports |
| Order-status / customer-service lookup | Help authorized users retrieve accurate order information |
| Sales and cross-branch dashboards | Standardize definitions and consolidate approved performance indicators |

Previous background notes remain in [40-Ideas](../40-Ideas/Ideas%20Index.md). Their prior cost-benefit scenarios are historical drafts and should not be treated as approved business cases.

The longer-term capability would prioritize opportunities collaboratively, measure current-state effort, test a manageable solution, and maintain those proven valuable.
