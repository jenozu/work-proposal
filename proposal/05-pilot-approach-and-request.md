# 5. Pilot Approach & Management Request

## Proposed Pilot Approach

Marindustrial can evaluate the proposed automation opportunities through a controlled, limited-scope pilot programme before committing to company-wide implementation or a permanent position. Management and process owners would select suitable workflows, agree on success criteria, document the current process, and identify the minimum software functionality needed for a fair comparison.

Initial testing would use representative transactions and, where appropriate, a SAP test environment or approved limited-access integration. Employees would review results, confirm correct transactions and document exceptions before any operational change is expanded. Projects could be tested in phases rather than launching all five simultaneously. A target or prototype must not be treated as an achieved result.

## Evaluation & Success Measures

For each selected process, the evaluation would record **current time versus assisted time**, typical workload, manual steps, correction and exception rates, accuracy, employee feedback, and impact during busy periods. Results would be annualized only using evidenced volumes and explicit assumptions. Development effort, integration requirements, security, training and ongoing support would also be documented. Where workflows overlap—such as RF picking, cycle counting and shipping—the same time improvement would not be counted more than once.

An end-of-pilot summary would identify what worked, what needs refinement, what it costs to maintain, and which opportunities warrant additional investment.

## Management Support Requested

Approval is requested for an **initial, supervised pilot evaluation** of selected priority processes, supported by:

- An agreed scope, success criteria, project sponsor and process owners.
- Reasonable time allocated for workflow measurement, development and testing.
- Appropriate access to SAP Business One data or a test environment, subject to IT, management and system-provider approval.
- Coordination with IT, the SAP provider and external systems such as Purolator where required.
- Participation by relevant employees to verify process accuracy, exceptions and practical usability.
- **Separate approval** before incurring new integration, hosting, software, equipment or other material expenses.

## Longer-Term Opportunity

The immediate decision is to authorize a practical evaluation—not to commit to all five deployments or approve a permanent role in advance. If the pilots demonstrate sufficient, repeatable value and manageable support costs, management could then consider creating a **dedicated internal Software Developer role** to design, deploy, integrate and maintain improvements across Marindustrial's branches and departments.
