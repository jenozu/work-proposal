# 5. Pilot Approach & Management Request

## Proposed Pilot Approach

Marindustrial can begin with a **proposed 60-day, supervised pilot evaluation** of one or more selected workflows. The duration is a working proposal, not an authorized deadline. Management and the responsible employees would agree on scope, measurable success criteria, source data, an operational owner and acceptable access before any integration or live process changes.

Initial development should use representative transactions and approved read-only access or a SAP test environment where practical. Pilot work can be phased rather than launching five applications at once. Correctness, permissions, exception handling, rollback and employee acceptance take priority over rapid automation. Any material paid services, SAP posting capabilities, relocation of inventory or customer-facing action must receive separate authorization.

## Evaluation & Success Measures

For every selected workflow, measure the complete **manual versus software-assisted process**, representative annual volume, corrections, exceptions, employee review, data quality and operating impact. Annualize only evidence-backed improvements and separately record build time, licences, integrations, devices, relocation, hosting, training and maintenance. Do not treat projected targets as achieved results.

Cycle counting requires Finance/audit review before reducing the annual physical count. Slotting should simulate and then carefully trial current-versus-proposed placements with real physical-pick events, accurate bin distances, safety constraints and relocation costs. Shipping requires correct labels, tracking and SAP documents. RF and slotting benefits must not credit the same walking reduction twice.

At the proposed evaluation's conclusion, summarize measured outcomes, risks, cost of upkeep and a recommendation to continue, revise, pause or stop each candidate.

## Management Support Requested

- Assign a sponsor, relevant process owners and a limited scope with acceptance criteria.
- Allow reasonable observation, development, testing and user-review time.
- Approve necessary system/data access through management, IT and SAP/carrier providers.
- Require safe test data, reconciliations, audit logging, user training and rollback plans.
- Approve new integration fees, equipment or hosting **separately** before expense is incurred.

## Longer-Term Opportunity

Approval now is for **pilot evaluation**, not for all five deployments or an automatic permanent position. If the work demonstrates sustainable, repeatable operational value, management can assess a dedicated internal **Software Developer** role to design, deploy, integrate and maintain improvements throughout Marindustrial.
