# 2. Opportunity Portfolio

The proposed internal software-development capability is designed for **continuous improvement across operations**, not only shipping or the warehouse. Software should complement SAP Business One and respect existing employees, processes and approvals.

| Area | Illustrative opportunities | Potential value |
|---|---|---|
| **Warehouse & Inventory** | ABC cycle counts, RF receiving/picking, location lookup, demand-led slotting and route simulation | Accuracy, continuity, reduced travel and fewer avoidable steps |
| **Shipping & Fulfillment** | Purolator batch creation, SAP delivery integration, labels and shipping notices | Reduced repetitive administration and more reliable records |
| **Reporting & Business Intelligence** | Service reports, scheduled KPI outputs and controlled management dashboards | Less manual compilation and more timely information |
| **Purchasing / SAP Administration** | Direct-ship workflows, vendor PDF retrieval, invoice matching, GRPO comparison, document attachments | Less duplicate entry, quicker controlled document handling |
| **Sales & Customer Service** | Order status, product information search, cross-sell analysis, customer follow-up | Better access to validated information |
| **Cross-Branch Operations** | Common definitions, role-based data access, auditable reusable integrations | Consistency and replication where processes genuinely align |

The **five current pilot opportunities** are cycle counting, reporting/BI, batch shipping, warehouse slotting and RF scanning/WMS. **Direct-Ship Processing & SAP Invoice Management** is a leading near-term backlog idea, with reportedly partial prior development; this does not establish production readiness or a measured financial benefit.

A dedicated developer could partner with process owners to **identify → measure → design → build → test → evaluate → maintain**. Actual priority should reflect workflow volume, employee experience, technical feasibility, data access, operating controls and net business value. The present request is for controlled evaluation; future AI agents/orchestration are a separate technical research option, not an approved management pilot or a reason to replace deterministic business logic.
