# Project 1 — Automated Inventory Cycle Counting

## Current Challenge

Marindustrial's annual inventory count has historically required approximately **four employees for two to three working days**, equivalent to about **64–96 direct employee-hours** at eight hours per day. The operational disruption can be more important than direct labour: routine warehouse activity is interrupted, while inventory errors may remain undiscovered between annual checks. Historical SAP count-line accuracy was reported at **97.36% in 2023**, **94.72% in 2024** and **91.41% in 2025**, with consistent definitions still to be confirmed.

## Proposed Solution

Introduce controlled ABC/risk-based counting integrated with approved SAP Business One data. Assign a manageable daily set of items/locations, provide appropriate localized count-area controls, record quantities, and route variances to authorized reviewers rather than posting unchecked adjustments. Current **provisional** intervals to evaluate are **A = 30, B = 90 and C = 365 calendar days**. These are **not a committed company policy** and should be calibrated against stock exposure, discrepancies, criticality, movement and practical count effort.

## Preliminary Analysis

A preliminary SAP study identified **3,833 stocked item records**; earlier partial classifications do not cover that entire universe and require reconciliation. Physical effort is not uniform. Concentrated engines and PTOs may be quick to verify together, bulky boxed components can sometimes be counted rapidly, and small fasteners may take longer to identify and count. A previous **15–20 minute daily physical-count** concept remains unvalidated, especially when preparation, travel, investigations and adjustments are considered.

## Expected Benefits

Earlier discrepancy detection, better inventory reliability and potentially less disruption from a multi-day annual count. The program could use **equal or greater total yearly labour** than the current count, so direct payroll savings are **not** assumed. Reduction or replacement of year-end counting must satisfy management, Finance and audit requirements.

## Pilot & Evaluation

Time representative A/B/C locations across item sizes and storage zones. Record preparation, movement controls, physical counting, variances, reviews, rechecks and effects on shipping/receiving. Validate item coverage, a workable calendar-to-workday schedule, accuracy and the actual total workload before setting final intervals.

## Primary Objective

Improve inventory accuracy and operational continuity with practical, controlled recurring checks, rather than merely reducing counted minutes.
