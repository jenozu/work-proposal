# 4. Estimated Cost-Benefit Summary

The five pilots address distinct challenges: accuracy, operational continuity, repeated administration, walking distance and warehouse throughput. Available working evidence does **not** support an additive cash-savings or ROI forecast. Employee time released is not automatically a reduction in payroll.

| Pilot | Available baseline and scenario | Expected value / open validation |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Cycle counting** | Annual physical count: about 4 employees for 2–3 eight-hour days (**64–96 direct person-hours**). Provisional ABC cadence: **30/90/365 calendar days** | Potentially less shutdown and earlier variance detection; **net labour savings not established**. Count coverage, physical item mix, exception labour and audit requirements must be tested. |
| **2. Reporting/BI** | One weekly service report estimated at **1–2 hours/week**, or **52–104 existing workload hours/year** | Net time saved is unknown until complete reporting, review, exceptions and maintenance are timed. |
| **3. Batch shipping/SAP** | **75 seconds/shipment** current; **10–15 seconds** assisted **target**. Historical review: **2,020 completed shipments** Oct 2025–Sep 2026 | If all were eligible and achieved target: **33.7–36.5 projected annual handling hours**, based on 60–65 seconds removed per shipment (**80.0–86.7%**). Actual eligibility, reliability and costs remain unverified. |
| **4. Warehouse slotting** | Five years of SAP records and bin data are candidate inputs; actual local pick frequency and routes must be validated | Possible reduced walking and full pick-to-stage time; **no verified percentage or annual savings**. Deduct relocation and disruption costs. |
| **5. RF Scanner/WMS** | An existing codebase is a development starting point; feature readiness needs verification | Potential accuracy and productivity gains only for distinct workflows, excluding time already credited to Projects 1, 3 or 4. |

**Shipping arithmetic:** 2,020 shipments × (60–65 seconds) ÷ 3,600 = **33.7–36.5 hours**. This remains a hypothetical full-eligibility scenario, not realized savings. **Reporting arithmetic:** 1–2 hours/week × 52 = **52–104 hours of current workload**, not proven savings. Do not add these different measures and describe the sum as achieved benefit.

Evaluation must include development, data access, SAP/carrier interfaces, licences, hosting, devices, relocation, security, testing, training and ongoing maintenance. Present time released, avoided expense, continuity and error-rate changes separately. A small automation may have modest direct dollar savings yet usefully demonstrate the ability to improve many repeated workflows; no speculative company-wide multiplier is claimed.
