# 4. Estimated Cost-Benefit Summary

The proposed applications address different sources of recurring work. Available observations support a preliminary comparison, but **they do not yet support a defensible portfolio-wide cash-savings or return-on-investment claim**. Time potentially released represents capacity that could be redirected to productive work, not an automatic reduction in payroll.

| Project | Available baseline or proposed target | Preliminary annual opportunity | Evidence / validation needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Inventory cycle counting** | Annual count: about four staff over 2–3 working days (64–96 employee-hours). Daily physical counting concept: roughly 15–20 min/day | Main value: reduce shutdown/disruption and identify discrepancies earlier; net labour savings **not established** | Pilot counts, investigations, staffing, variance and year-end reconciliation |
| **2. Centralized reporting** | One identified service report takes an estimated 1–2 hours per week | **52–104 hours/year of existing manual workload exposed to automation**, not guaranteed hours saved | Timed before/after reporting process; review and exception time |
| **3. Batch shipping + SAP** | Approx. 75 sec/shipment currently; 10–15 sec/shipment **target**; possible reduction 60–65 sec | At **5–10 shipments/day × 250 days**, approx. **21–45 hours/year** of potential capacity released; assumed volume | Actual annual shipments, complete end-to-end trials, failure handling |
| **4. Direct-ship + invoice management** | Repetitive interbranch and SAP document steps observed; no verified time-per-case baseline | **TBD** | Separate time studies and transaction volumes for both sub-workflows |
| **5. RF Scanner / WMS** | Existing development/prototypes; receiving/picking/lookup/staging improvement potential | **TBD**; count only improvements incremental to Projects 1 and 3 | Controlled task trials, errors, adoption, support effort |

**Illustrative combined range:** The reporting workload identified above (**52–104 hours/year**) plus the shipping scenario (**approximately 21–45 hours/year**) equals **roughly 73–149 hours/year potentially addressed** under the stated assumptions. This is **not** an observed combined saving; reporting automation may not eliminate all preparation or review, and shipping volumes and realized processing improvements require validation. It excludes the other three projects and must not be presented as total company savings.

Actual costs remain to be scoped: development and integration effort, SAP/provider access, software or carrier interfaces, testing, security, training, hosting, ongoing support and maintenance. Avoided expense, released employee time, improved accuracy, faster throughput and reduced downtime are different benefit categories and must be reported separately.

These projects provide a credible basis for **measurement-led pilot testing**, after which management can compare realized operational value with full build-and-support costs and decide which improvements merit investment.
