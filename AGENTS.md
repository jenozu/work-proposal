# Coding Agent Instructions — work-proposal

## Repository role

This is Marindustrial's **pre-proposal and evidence knowledge base**, not a production SAP, carrier or warehouse application. Canonical management material: proposal/; evidence: evidence/; exploratory AI research: notebooklm/. The consolidated proposal/complete-pre-proposal.md is a generated reading copy to refresh after source changes.

## Branch safety

- **Do not change, push, force-push or merge into main** without explicit user authorization.
- Create or reuse a descriptive feature branch. Draft PRs are for review, not auto-merge.
- Preserve legacy notes for context; do not make the former 13-section roadmap canonical.
- Keep the five current pilot names stable; Direct-Ship/SAP Invoice Management belongs to Tier 1 backlog, not Pilot 4.

## Evidence rules

- Distinguish observed, historical, estimated/scenario, target and unknown.
- No invented baseline, implementation status, saving, source data or ROI.
- A/B/C **30/90/365 calendar-day** intervals are **provisional** and pending pilot verification.
- Slotting must prioritize **local physical pick frequency**, not revenue or total units sold; exclude direct-ship events.
- Attribute overlapping travel, RF, shipping and counting gains only once.
- Management pilots are not authorized production deployments; don't call draft goals results.

## Security and confidentiality

This is a **public repo**. Do not add company raw SAP exports, customer/employee info, proprietary pricing, invoices, supplier documents, secrets, internal credentials or screenshots without authorization. Do not execute autonomous write changes to SAP, carrier systems, inventory or customer communications. Begin AI orchestration exploration with synthetic/read-only tools and design human approval, logging, least privilege and rollback.

When changing any proposal section, pilot or appendix, synchronize proposal/complete-pre-proposal.md and verify relative links.
